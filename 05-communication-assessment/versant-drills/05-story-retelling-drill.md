# Versant Communication Assessment: Section 5 — Story Retelling Drill

## What this is
Section 5 of the Accenture Communication Assessment plays a short audio story (~3 to 5 sentences long) **twice**. After the second reading, a beep sounds, and you have **30 seconds** to retell the story in your own words into your microphone.

---

## Formula / Rule / Pattern

```
[Audio Story Read Twice] ──> Extract 4 Anchors ──> [30s Spoken Retelling]
```

**The 4 Story Anchors**:
1. **Who**: Main character / protagonist (e.g. *Mark, a software engineer*).
2. **What**: The main event / problem encountered (e.g. *his laptop crashed before a presentation*).
3. **Where / When**: Setting / location (e.g. *at the office on Tuesday morning*).
4. **Outcome**: How the situation was resolved (e.g. *he restored a backup from the cloud and delivered a successful talk*).

---

## Shortcut: The 4-Anchor Tag Retelling Framework

> [!TIP]
> ### 4-Anchor Tag Trick
> Mentally tag the 4 anchors (**Who**, **What**, **Where/When**, **Outcome**) as you listen during the second audio reading. Speak from those 4 anchors rather than trying to memorize exact word-for-word phrasing.
> 
> *Why it works*: The AI grading engine evaluates your summary against key plot points and narrative coherence, not verbatim memorization. Covering all 4 anchors guarantees a 100% content score.

## 10 Short Practice Stories & 4-Anchor Templates

### Story 1: The Lost Keys
- **Audio Story**:  
  *"Sarah was getting ready to leave for an important job interview at nine in the morning. As she walked to her car, she realized she had left her car keys inside her apartment. Fortunately, her roommate was still home and unlocked the door for her. Sarah arrived at the interview with five minutes to spare."*
- **4-Anchor Tag Summary**:
  - *Who*: Sarah
  - *What*: Forgot car keys inside apartment before a job interview
  - *Where/When*: 9 AM at her apartment
  - *Outcome*: Roommate unlocked the door, Sarah arrived on time
- **Sample 30-Second Spoken Retelling**:  
  *"This story is about Sarah, who was leaving for a job interview at nine in the morning. She accidentally left her car keys inside her apartment. Luckily, her roommate was home and unlocked the door for her, allowing Sarah to reach her interview on time."*

### Story 2: The Bakery Order
- **Audio Story**:  
  *"David ordered a custom birthday cake from a local bakery for his daughter's tenth birthday party. When he picked up the cake on Saturday, he noticed the name on the icing was misspelled. The baker apologized and corrected the spelling within fifteen minutes. The party was a great success."*
- **4-Anchor Tags**:
  - *Who*: David and his daughter
  - *What*: Misspelled name on birthday cake
  - *Where/When*: Local bakery on Saturday
  - *Outcome*: Baker corrected it in 15 minutes, party was successful

## Practice Questions & Scoring Rules

Q1. How many times is the short audio story read aloud in Section 5 of Versant?  
a) Once  
b) Twice  
c) Three times  
d) Four times  

Q2. What are the 4 anchor elements every story retelling must include?  
a) Title, Word Count, Grammar, Font  
b) Who (Character), What (Problem), Where/When (Setting), Outcome (Resolution)  
c) Date, Time, Price, Author  

Q3. What is the response time window for speaking your story retelling?  
a) 10 seconds  
b) 30 seconds  
c) 60 seconds  
d) 5 minutes  

---

## Answers

1. **b) Twice** — Played twice back-to-back.
2. **b) Who, What, Where/When, Outcome** — The 4-Anchor plot summary model.
3. **b) 30 seconds** — Standard response window.
