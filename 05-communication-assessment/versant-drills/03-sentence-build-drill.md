# Versant Communication Assessment: Section 3 — Sentence Build Drill

## What this is
Section 3 of the Accenture Communication Assessment plays **~10 scrambled audio phrase fragments** (e.g. *"was late" ... "the train" ... "because of rain"*). You must instantly reorder the fragments in your mind and speak the grammatically correct, complete sentence smoothly into your microphone.

---

## Formula / Rule / Pattern

```
[Fragment A: "was late"] + [Fragment B: "the train"] + [Fragment C: "because of rain"]
                          ↓
   Reconstruct: [Subject: "the train"] + [Verb: "was late"] + [Reason: "because of rain"]
                          ↓
   Spoken Result: "The train was late because of rain."
```

---

## Shortcut: Find the Anchor Subject First

> [!TIP]
> ### Anchor Subject First
> Identify the **Noun / Subject fragment** among the 3 played phrases first. Place it at the start, follow it with the **Verb phrase**, and attach the **Prepositional / Time phrase** at the end.
> 
> *Why it works*: English sentences follow strict `Subject $\rightarrow$ Verb $\rightarrow$ Object` topology. Pinpointing the subject locks in 50% of the sentence structure instantly.

## Practice Questions

Q1. What is the standard number of scrambled fragments played per question in Sentence Build?  
a) 2  
b) 3  
c) 5  
d) 7  

Q2. What is the fundamental English word order rule used to solve Sentence Builds?  
a) Subject + Verb + Object  
b) Verb + Object + Subject  
c) Preposition + Adjective + Verb  
d) Random order  

Q3. What should you do if you stutter while speaking the reconstructed sentence?  
a) Pause, take a deep breath, and finish fluently without stopping to restart  
b) Scream  
c) Hang up  
d) Press backspace  

---

## Answers

1. **b) 3** — Standard Versant scrambled fragment count.
2. **a) Subject + Verb + Object** — Core English syntax topology.
3. **a) Pause, take a deep breath, and finish fluently...** — Fluency retention strategy.
