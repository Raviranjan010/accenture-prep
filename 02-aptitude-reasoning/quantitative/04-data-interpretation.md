# Data Interpretation — Charts, Graphs & Tables

## What this is
Data Interpretation (DI) tests your ability to extract quantitative facts, compute percentage growths, analyze ratios, and synthesize information presented in structured formats such as Bar Graphs, Pie Charts, Line Charts, and Data Tables. It measures analytical speed under time constraints.

---

## Formula / Rule / Pattern

| Formula Name | Mathematical Formula | Usage Context |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Percentage Increase / Decrease** | $\frac{\text{Value}_{\text{New}} - \text{Value}_{\text{Base}}}{\text{Value}_{\text{Base}}} \times 100$ | Growth / Decline analysis |
| **Percentage Share of Total** | $\frac{\text{Category Value}}{\text{Total Sum}} \times 100$ | Pie chart & Bar breakdown |
| **Pie Chart Degree to % Conversion** | $\text{Percentage} = \frac{\text{Angle in Degrees}}{360^\circ} \times 100$ | Converting sector angles to % |
| **Pie Chart % to Degree Conversion** | $\text{Angle in Degrees} = \frac{\text{Percentage}}{100} \times 360^\circ$ | Converting % to sector angle |

---

## Shortcuts & Tricks

### Shortcut 1: The Raw Difference Baseline Trick
> [!TIP]
> For **"What % more or less is X than Y?"** questions, calculate the **raw difference ($|X - Y|$) first**, then divide by the base value ($Y$). Do NOT compute $X\%$ of total and $Y\%$ of total separately before subtracting.
> 
> *Why it works*: Dividing a single subtracted term $\frac{X - Y}{Y}$ requires 1 division step, whereas computing $\frac{X}{\text{Total}} - \frac{Y}{\text{Total}}$ requires 2 divisions and a subtraction step, doubling your work and error potential.

### Shortcut 2: Pie Chart Angle Anchors
> [!TIP]
> Memorize key **Angle-to-Percentage Benchmark Anchors**:
> - $36^\circ = 10\%$
> - $72^\circ = 20\%$
> - $90^\circ = 25\%$
> - $180^\circ = 50\%$
> 
> *Why it works*: Allows instant visual estimation during pie chart questions without performing full 360-degree multiplications.

## Worked Examples

### Example 1: Table DI (Easy)
- **Table Data**: Revenue of 3 branches (in Lakhs):
  - 2023: Branch A = 40, Branch B = 50, Branch C = 60 (Total = 150)
  - 2024: Branch A = 50, Branch B = 65, Branch C = 85 (Total = 200)
- **Question**: What is the percentage increase in total revenue from 2023 to 2024?
- **Step-by-step Solution**:
  1. Base Value (2023) $= 150$. New Value (2024) $= 200$.
  2. Difference $= 200 - 150 = 50$.
  3. $\% \text{ Increase} = \frac{50}{150} \times 100 = \frac{1}{3} \times 100 = 33.33\%$.

### Example 2: Pie Chart Angle Conversion (Medium)
- **Question**: A company expense pie chart shows IT Infrastructure at an angle of $54^\circ$. Total expenditure is ₹20 Lakhs. What is the amount spent on IT Infrastructure?
- **Step-by-step Solution**:
  1. Use Angle Anchor: $36^\circ = 10\% \implies 18^\circ = 5\%$. So $54^\circ = 15\%$.
  2. Amount $= 15\% \text{ of } 20 \text{ Lakhs} = 0.15 \times 20 = ₹3 \text{ Lakhs}$.

### Example 3: Bar Graph Comparative % Growth (Hard)
- **Data**:
  - Company X Sales: 2021 = 400 units, 2022 = 520 units.
  - Company Y Sales: 2021 = 300 units, 2022 = 420 units.
- **Question**: Which company had a higher percentage growth in sales from 2021 to 2022, and by how much?
- **Step-by-step Solution**:
  1. Company X Growth $= \frac{520 - 400}{400} \times 100 = \frac{120}{400} \times 100 = 30\%$.
  2. Company Y Growth $= \frac{420 - 300}{300} \times 100 = \frac{120}{300} \times 100 = 40\%$.
  3. **Comparison**: Company Y had a higher growth by $40\% - 30\% = 10\%$ percentage points higher.

## Answers

1. **a) 198 Crores** — $(120+150+180+240+300)/5 = 990/5 = 198$.
2. **b) 60%** — Growth $= (240 - 150)/150 \times 100 = 90/150 \times 100 = 60\%$.
3. **c) 2023 and 2024 (Tie at 60)** — $2023 - 2022 = 240 - 180 = 60$; $2024 - 2023 = 300 - 240 = 60$.
4. **a) 2:5** — $120:300 = 12:30 = 2:5$.
5. **b) 360 Crores** — $300 \times 1.20 = 360$.
6. **a) ₹18,000** — $108^\circ / 360^\circ = 30\%$. $30\% \text{ of } 60,000 = ₹18,000$.
7. **b) 20%** — $72^\circ / 360^\circ = 20\%$.
8. **b) 5:3** — Food ($90^\circ$) : Education ($54^\circ$) $= 90:54 = 5:3$.
9. **b) ₹3,000** — Angle difference $= 108^\circ - 90^\circ = 18^\circ = 5\%$. $5\% \text{ of } 60,000 = ₹3,000$.
10. **b) $64.8^\circ$** — New angle $= 54^\circ \times 1.20 = 64.8^\circ$.
11. **c) Student C** — Total 270 (Average = 90).
12. **b) 76.67** — $(80 + 60 + 90)/3 = 230/3 = 76.67$.
13. **b) 28.57%** — $(270 - 210)/210 \times 100 = 60/210 \times 100 = 28.57\%$.
14. **a) 245:245 (1:1)** — Total Sci $= 90+70+85 = 245$. Total Eng $= 70+80+95 = 245$. Ratio $= 1:1$.
15. **a) Avoids computing individual absolute percentages separately** — Saves computation steps.
