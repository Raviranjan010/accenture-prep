# Versant Communication Assessment: Section 4 — Short Answer Questions

## What this is
Section 4 of the Accenture Communication Assessment asks **~24 rapid-fire audio questions** testing basic vocabulary, general knowledge, and listening comprehension. You have 3-5 seconds to reply with a **1-to-2 word direct spoken answer**.

---

## Formula / Rule / Pattern

```
[Audio Question: "What do we use to cut paper?"] ──> [Instant 1-Word Answer: "Scissors"]
```

- **Rule**: Keep answers strictly to **one or two words**. Do NOT answer in full long sentences (e.g. say *"Scissors"*, not *"We use a pair of scissors to cut paper"*).
- **Time Window**: 3 seconds response window per question.

---

## 24 Rapid Short Answer Questions (Drill Bank)

1. **Q1**: What instrument do we use to tell time?  
   - **Spoken Answer**: *"Clock"* / *"Watch"*

2. **Q2**: What do you call a person who flies an airplane?  
   - **Spoken Answer**: *"Pilot"*

3. **Q3**: Which season comes right after winter?  
   - **Spoken Answer**: *"Spring"*

4. **Q4**: What do we use to freeze food and keep it cold?

5. **Q5**: Is a dog a mammal or a reptile?  
   - **Spoken Answer**: *"Mammal"*

6. **Q6**: What document do you need to travel internationally to another country?  
   - **Spoken Answer**: *"Passport"*

7. **Q7**: What is the primary language spoken in France?

10. **Q10**: Which organ in the human body pumps blood throughout the circulatory system?

11. **Q11**: If something is not cheap, it is...?

12. **Q12**: What do we wear on our hands to keep them warm in winter?

13. **Q13**: What is the opposite of the word "heavy"?

14. **Q14**: What do we call a period of ten years?

15. **Q15**: What liquid falls from clouds when it rains?

18. **Q18**: What is the main source of light for planet Earth?

19. **Q19**: What room in a house is used for cooking meals?

20. **Q20**: If you add 5 and 5, what is the total?

21. **Q21**: What do we call a practitioner who treats sick people in a hospital?

## Practice Questions & Rules

Q1. How long should your response be in the Short Answer Questions section?  
a) A full 3-sentence paragraph  
b) 1 to 2 direct words  
c) 10 words  
d) Silence  

Q2. What happens if you pause for 4 seconds before answering?  
a) The AI moves to the next question automatically, scoring the item as unattempted  
b) You get extra points  
c) The question repeats  
d) The timer pauses  

---

## Answers

1. **b) 1 to 2 direct words** — Direct short answer requirement.
2. **a) The AI moves to the next question automatically...** — Timeout mechanism.
