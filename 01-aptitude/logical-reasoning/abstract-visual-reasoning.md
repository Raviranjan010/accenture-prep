# Abstract & Visual Reasoning — Series & Spatial Patterns

## What this is
Abstract and Visual Reasoning evaluates your non-verbal cognitive ability to identify logical patterns, geometric rotations, visual transformations, matrix progressions, and sequence completions across graphical shapes without relies on words or numbers. It measures fluid intelligence, spatial visualization, and inductive reasoning speed.

---

## Formula / Rule / Pattern

| Transformation Type | Visual Mechanism | Typical Sequence Rule |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Rotation** | Shape rotates around its center | Clockwise (CW) or Counter-Clockwise (CCW) in 45°, 90°, or 135° steps |
| **Reflection / Mirror** | Image flipped across axis | Horizontal flip (Left-Right) or Vertical flip (Top-Bottom) |
| **Element Addition / Removal** | Number of lines/dots change | $+1, +2, +3$ elements added sequentially per step |
| **Shading / Inversion** | Fill pattern alternates | White $\rightarrow$ Black $\rightarrow$ Striped $\rightarrow$ White |

---

## Shortcut: The Fixed Order Rule Inspection Sequence

> [!TIP]
> ### Fixed Order Rule Inspection Trick
> When presented with a complex visual pattern series, check for visual transformation rules strictly in this fixed 4-step sequence:
> 
> $$\text{1. Rotation} \longrightarrow \text{2. Reflection} \longrightarrow \text{3. Element Count} \longrightarrow \text{4. Shading Change}$$
> 
> *Why it works*: Most visual reasoning test items employ **only one primary transformation rule** or at most two layered rules. Checking transformation rules in a systematic fixed order prevents visual cognitive fatigue and stops you from overanalyzing multiple complex hypotheses simultaneously.

## Worked Examples

### Example 1: Rotational Series (Easy)
- **Question**: A clock arrow points North in Frame 1, East in Frame 2, South in Frame 3. What direction will it point in Frame 4?
- **Step-by-step Solution**:
  1. Check Rotation: Arrow turns $90^\circ$ Clockwise per step.
  2. Frame 1 (North) $+ 90^\circ \implies$ East (Frame 2).
  3. Frame 2 (East) $+ 90^\circ \implies$ South (Frame 3).
  4. Frame 3 (South) $+ 90^\circ \implies$ **West (Frame 4)**.

### Example 2: Element Count & Shading Matrix (Medium)
- **Question**: Frame 1 has 3 white circles. Frame 2 has 4 black circles. Frame 3 has 5 white circles. What does Frame 4 contain?
- **Step-by-step Solution**:
  1. Check Element Count: $+1$ circle per step ($3 \rightarrow 4 \rightarrow 5 \rightarrow 6$).
  2. Check Shading: Alternates (White $\rightarrow$ Black $\rightarrow$ White $\rightarrow$ Black).
  3. **Answer**: Frame 4 contains **6 black circles**.

### Example 3: Dual Rule Layered Transformation (Hard)
- **Question**: Frame 1 has an L-shape in top-left with 1 dot inside. Frame 2 has L-shape rotated $90^\circ$ CW to top-right with 2 dots. Frame 3 has L-shape in bottom-right with 3 dots. Describe Frame 4.
- **Step-by-step Solution**:
  1. Rotation/Position: Moves around 4 corners CW (Top-Left $\rightarrow$ Top-Right $\rightarrow$ Bottom-Right $\rightarrow$ **Bottom-Left**).
  2. Orientation: Rotates $90^\circ$ CW per corner shift.
  3. Dots count: Increases by $+1$ ($1 \rightarrow 2 \rightarrow 3 \rightarrow 4$).
  4. **Answer**: L-shape in **Bottom-Left corner**, rotated $270^\circ$ CW from original, containing **4 dots**.

## Practice Questions (PYQ Bank)

Q1. A square has a dot at its top-left corner. Next frame: top-right. Next frame: bottom-right. Where is the dot in the next frame?  
a) Top-left  
b) Bottom-left  
c) Center  
d) Bottom-right  

Q2. An equilateral triangle points UP in Step 1, DOWN in Step 2, UP in Step 3. What orientation is Step 4?  
a) Right  
b) Left  
c) DOWN  
d) UP  

Q3. Figure A has 3 sides (triangle). Figure B has 4 sides (quadrilateral). Figure C has 5 sides (pentagon). What shape comes next?  
a) Circle  
b) Hexagon (6 sides)  
c) Heptagon  
d) Octagon  

Q4. In a $3 \times 3$ visual matrix, row 1 contains 1 line, 2 lines, 3 lines. Row 2 contains 2 lines, 3 lines, 4 lines. Row 3 contains 3 lines, 4 lines, ?  
a) 4 lines  
b) 5 lines  
c) 6 lines  
d) 2 lines  

Q5. A black star alternates between being inside a circle and outside a circle. In frame 5, it is inside. Where is it in frame 6?  
a) Inside the circle  
b) Outside the circle  
c) Inside a square  
d) Disappears  

Q6. A figure rotates $45^\circ$ Counter-Clockwise in each consecutive step. After 4 steps, what is its total net rotation?  
a) $180^\circ$ Counter-Clockwise  
b) $90^\circ$ Clockwise  
c) $360^\circ$  
d) $135^\circ$ Counter-Clockwise  

Q7. If an arrow pointing North is mirrored horizontally (reflected across vertical y-axis), which direction does the reflected arrow point?  
a) South  
b) East  
c) North  
d) West  

Q8. If an arrow pointing East is mirrored horizontally (reflected across vertical y-axis), which direction does it point?  
a) West  
b) East  
c) North  
d) South  

Q9. In a visual sequence, shading changes from White $\rightarrow$ Striped $\rightarrow$ Black $\rightarrow$ White. What is the shading in frame 7?  
a) White  
b) Striped  
c) Black  
d) Grey  

Q10. Figure 1: 1 vertical line. Figure 2: 1 vertical + 1 horizontal line (+ shape). Figure 3: + shape with diagonal line. Figure 4: * shape (4 intersecting lines). How many line segments are added per step?  
a) 1 line segment per step  
b) 2 line segments per step  
c) Alternating 1 and 2  
d) Exponential doubling  

Q11. A outer square contains an inner triangle. In the next frame, the outer shape becomes a triangle and the inner shape becomes a square. What transformation occurred?  
a) Inversion / Swap of inner and outer elements  
b) Rotation by $180^\circ$  
c) Reflection across x-axis  
d) Deletion of shapes  

Q12. What is the total angle rotation required for an asymmetrical object to return to its original exact orientation?  
a) $90^\circ$  
b) $180^\circ$  
c) $270^\circ$  
d) $360^\circ$  

Q13. In a grid sequence, a dot moves 1 step Clockwise along the perimeter of a 4x4 grid in each frame. How many total frames does it take to return to its starting corner?  
a) 4 frames  
b) 8 frames  
c) 12 frames  
d) 16 frames  

Q14. A line segment length doubles in each frame: 1cm $\rightarrow$ 2cm $\rightarrow$ 4cm $\rightarrow$ 8cm. What will be the length in frame 6?  
a) 16cm  
b) 32cm  
c) 64cm  
d) 128cm  

Q15. Why should you inspect Rotation before Shading in abstract reasoning tests?  
a) Rotation rules are more visually distinct and eliminate wrong options faster  
b) Shading is illegal  
c) Computers ignore shading  
d) Rotation is always $90^\circ$

## Answers

1. **b) Bottom-left** — Clockwise corner movement: TL $\rightarrow$ TR $\rightarrow$ BR $\rightarrow$ BL.
2. **c) DOWN** — Alternates UP / DOWN.
3. **b) Hexagon (6 sides)** — Sequence of polygon sides $+1$: $3 \rightarrow 4 \rightarrow 5 \rightarrow 6$.
4. **b) 5 lines** — Pattern across rows is $+1$ line count. $3, 4, 5$.
5. **b) Outside the circle** — Alternating position: Inside/Outside.
6. **a) $180^\circ$ Counter-Clockwise** — $4 \times 45^\circ = 180^\circ$ CCW.
7. **c) North** — Vertical line reflection leaves vertical vectors unchanged.
8. **a) West** — Reflecting horizontal East vector across y-axis flips it to West.
9. **a) White** — Cycle length = 3. Frame 1=White, 4=White, 7=White.
10. **a) 1 line segment per step** — $+1$ line added continuously.
11. **a) Inversion / Swap of inner and outer elements** — Element role reversal.
12. **d) $360^\circ$** — Full circle rotation returns to initial state.
13. **c) 12 frames** — Perimeter of 4x4 grid has $4+3+3+2 = 12$ perimeter steps.
14. **b) 32cm** — Geometric progression: Frame 1=1, 2=2, 3=4, 4=8, 5=16, 6=32cm.
15. **a) Rotation rules are more visually distinct...** — Faster structural elimination.
