# Gamified Assessment: Quick-Fire Math (Select Bubbles)

## What this is
Quick-Fire Math (also known as Select Bubbles or Numerical Sprint) is a high-speed numerical processing mini-game in the Accenture 2026 Gamified Assessment. Multiple floating or static numbered bubbles containing mathematical expressions (e.g. `14 + 18`, `45 / 3`, `7 x 6`, `30% of 120`) appear on the screen. You must select, rank, or pop the bubbles in strict ascending/descending order of their evaluated numerical value within tight 3-to-5 second round limits.

---

## Formula / Rule / Pattern

```
Bubble A: 14 + 18 = 32
Bubble B: 45 / 3  = 15
Bubble C: 7 x 6   = 42
Bubble D: 30% of 120 = 36

Ascending Order Selection: Bubble B (15) ──> Bubble A (32) ──> Bubble D (36) ──> Bubble C (42)
```

---

## Shortcut: The Nearest-10 Rounding & Benchmark Elimination Trick

> [!TIP]
> ### Nearest-10 Rounding Trick
> Round each expression to the nearest 10 or benchmark fraction first to eliminate 1–2 obviously smaller or larger bubbles **before** doing exact mental arithmetic on the remaining close pair.
> 
> *Why it works*: Full calculation precision is only required to resolve the tie-breaking pair, not all 4–5 bubbles. For example, comparing `7 x 6` (approx ~40) and `45 / 3` (approx ~15) requires zero exact math to know `15 < 40`. Eyeballing benchmark ranges saves 60% of calculation time per round.

## Worked Examples & Practice Scenarios

### Scenario 1 (3-Bubble Ascending Order)
- **Bubbles**: $A = 12 \times 4$, $B = 75 / 5$, $C = 19 + 24$.
- **Quick Evaluation**:
  - $A = 48$
  - $B = 15$
  - $C = 43$
- **Ascending Selection**: $B (15) \rightarrow C (43) \rightarrow A (48)$.
- **Time Taken**: 2.5 seconds.

### Scenario 2 (4-Bubble Descending Order with Percentages)
- **Bubbles**: $A = 25\% \text{ of } 160$, $B = 6^2$, $C = 11 \times 3$, $D = 100 - 62$.
- **Quick Evaluation**:
  - $A = 40$
  - $B = 36$
  - $C = 33$
  - $D = 38$
- **Descending Selection**: $A (40) \rightarrow D (38) \rightarrow B (36) \rightarrow C (33)$.

### Scenario 3 (Complex Operator Comparison under High Speed)
- **Bubbles**: $A = 14 \times 3 - 5$, $B = 88 / 2$, $C = 15 + 17 + 13$, $D = 5^2 + 18$.
- **Quick Evaluation**:
  - $A = 42 - 5 = 37$
  - $B = 44$
  - $C = 45$
  - $D = 25 + 18 = 43$
- **Ascending Order**: $A (37) \rightarrow D (43) \rightarrow B (44) \rightarrow C (45)$.

## Answers

1. **b) B (40)** — $120/3 = 40$. (A=64, C=42, D=45).
2. **b) 70** — $0.35 \times 200 = 70$.
3. **a) B, C, A, D** — B(45), C(52), A(56), D(64).
4. **c) $300 / 3$** — $300/3 = 100$. (A=90, B=94, D=88).
5. **b) 80** — $18 + 27 + 35 = 80$.
6. **d) Both a and b** — $14 \times 3 + 4 = 46 + 2? \implies 14 \times 3 = 42 + 4 = 46$. Wait! $144/3 = 48$. Let's re-verify: $14 \times 3 + 6 = 48$. $144/3 = 48 \implies$ Answer is **b) $144/3$**.
7. **a) It eliminates obvious non-candidates...** — Eyeballing speeds up selection.
8. **c) C (82)** — $33 + 49 = 82$. (A=81, B=80, D=75).
9. **a) 75** — $90 - 15 = 75$.
10. **a) B, A, C** — B(16), A(15), C(13).
11. **d) All of the above** — $6 \times 6 = 36$; $72/2 = 36$; $1.2 \times 30 = 36$.
12. **c) C (50)** — $200/4 = 50$.
13. **a) 42** — $25 + 17 = 42$.
14. **a) 2 $\rightarrow$ 1 $\rightarrow$ 3** — $42 < 45 < 49$.
15. **b) Rapid numerical agility and executive decision speed** — Official test objective.
