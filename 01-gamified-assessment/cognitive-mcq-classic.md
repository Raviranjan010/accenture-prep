# Gamified Assessment: Classic Cognitive MCQ Format Backup

## What this is
While most Accenture 2026 campus and off-campus hiring drives have transitioned to the interactive Gamified Assessment (Memory Maze, Path Finder, Quick-Fire Math), certain regional drives or off-campus recruitment portals still utilize the **Classic 3-Part Cognitive MCQ Test**. This module provides a complete reference guide and practice setup for the classic format across Numerical Ability, Verbal Ability, and Logical Reasoning.

---

## Formula / Rule / Pattern

| Section | Questions | Duration | Focus Areas |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Numerical Ability** | 15 Questions | ~20 mins | Percentages, Profit/Loss, Ratios, Speed-Time, Averages |
| **Logical Reasoning** | 15 Questions | ~20 mins | Blood Relations, Coding-Decoding, Seating, Series |
| **Verbal Ability** | 15 Questions | ~15 mins | Reading Comprehension, Para-jumbles, Grammar Error Spotting |

---

## Shortcut: The Section-Level Time-Cap Rule

> [!TIP]
> ### 60-Second Cut-and-Skip Rule
> In the classic MCQ round, never spend more than **60 seconds on any single question**. If a logical arrangement or algebra question is not solved within 45 seconds, mark an educated elimination guess and skip to the next item immediately.
> 
> *Why it works*: Accentures classic test has NO negative marking. Spending 3 minutes on 1 tough seating arrangement loses you 2 easy speed-math questions at the end of the test section.

## Worked Examples

### Example 1: Classic Numerical MCQ (Easy)
- **Question**: A car covers 180 km in 3 hours. How long will it take to cover 300 km at the same speed?
- **Step-by-step Solution**:
  1. Speed $= 180 / 3 = 60$ km/h.
  2. Time required $= \text{Distance} / \text{Speed} = 300 / 60 = 5$ hours.

### Example 2: Classic Logical MCQ (Medium)
- **Question**: Pointing to a photograph, Rohit said, "She is the daughter of my grandfather's only son." How is the woman related to Rohit?
- **Step-by-step Solution**:
  1. "Grandfather's only son" $\implies$ Rohit's father.
  2. "Daughter of my father" $\implies$ Rohit's sister.
  3. **Answer**: Sister.

### Example 3: Classic Verbal MCQ (Hard)
- **Question**: Rearrange the sentences: (P) which is essential for human survival. (Q) Water is a finite resource (R) and must be conserved diligently (S) across all global communities.
- **Step-by-step Solution**:
  1. Identify subject: Q ("Water is a finite resource").
  2. Relative clause P modifies resource ("which is essential...").
  3. Action clause R ("and must be conserved...") followed by location S.
  4. Sequence: **Q $\rightarrow$ P $\rightarrow$ R $\rightarrow$ S**.

## Practice Questions (PYQ Bank)

Q1. If $A:B = 3:4$ and $B:C = 8:9$, find $A:C$.  
a) 1:2  
b) 2:3  
c) 3:2  
d) 4:5  

Q2. Find the missing term in the series: 4, 9, 19, 39, 79, ?  
a) 119  
b) 139  
c) 159  
d) 169  

Q3. In a certain code, `TEACHER` is written as `VGCEJGT`. How is `STUDENT` written in that code?  
a) UVWFGPU  
b) TVWFGPU  
c) UVWGEPV  
d) UVWGEPU  

Q4. A train 150m long passes a pole in 15 seconds. What is the speed of the train in km/h?  
a) 30 km/h  
b) 36 km/h  
c) 40 km/h  
d) 45 km/h  

Q5. Select the antonym of **"MITIGATE"**:  
a) Relieve  
b) Aggravate  
c) Diminish  
d) Alleviate  

Q6. Average of 5 numbers is 27. If one number is excluded, average becomes 25. What is the excluded number?  
a) 30  
b) 35  
c) 40  
d) 25  

Q7. If $P + Q$ means $P$ is father of $Q$, $P - Q$ means $P$ is sister of $Q$, what does $A + B - C$ mean?  
a) A is uncle of C  
b) A is father of C  
c) A is brother of C  
d) A is grandfather of C  

Q8. Complete the sentence: "Neither the manager nor the employees _____ present at the meeting."  
a) was  
b) were  
c) is  
d) has been  

Q9. Cost price of 15 articles is equal to selling price of 12 articles. Find profit percentage.  
a) 20%  
b) 25%  
c) 30%  
d) 15%  

Q10. Five friends A, B, C, D, E sit in a row facing North. C sits in the middle. A sits at the left end. B is to the immediate right of C. Who sits at the right end if D is between A and C?  
a) B  
b) D  
c) E  
d) C  

Q11. Pick the correctly spelt word:  
a) Commitee  
b) Committee  
c) Comittee  
d) Commitey  

Q12. What is 25% of 40% of 500?  
a) 40  
b) 50  
c) 60  
d) 100  

Q13. Statement: All cats are dogs. All dogs are birds. Conclusion I: All cats are birds. Conclusion II: Some birds are cats.  
a) Only I follows  
b) Only II follows  
c) Both I and II follow  
d) Neither follows  

Q14. Synonym of **"METICULOUS"**:  
a) Careless  
b) Painstaking / Careful  
c) Rapid  
d) Bold  

Q15. Solve: $x^2 - 7x + 12 = 0$. Roots are:  
a) 3, 4  
b) -3, -4  
c) 2, 6  
d) 1, 12  

---

## Answers

1. **b) 2:3** — $A/C = (3/4) \times (8/9) = 24/36 = 2/3$.
2. **c) 159** — Pattern: $\times 2 + 1$. ($79 \times 2 + 1 = 159$).
3. **c) UVWGEPV** — Shift $+2$ for each letter.
4. **b) 36 km/h** — Speed $= 150/15 = 10$ m/s $= 10 \times 18/5 = 36$ km/h.
5. **b) Aggravate** — Mitigate means reduce; aggravate means make worse.
6. **b) 35** — Excluded $= (5 \times 27) - (4 \times 25) = 135 - 100 = 35$.
7. **b) A is father of C** — A is father of B; B is sister of C $\implies$ A is father of C.
8. **b) were** — Verb agrees with closer subject ("employees" = plural).
9. **b) 25%** — Profit $\% = (15-12)/12 \times 100 = 3/12 \times 100 = 25\%$.
10. **c) E** — Row order: A, D, C, B, E. E is at right end.
11. **b) Committee** — Double m, double t, double e.
12. **b) 50** — $0.25 \times 0.40 \times 500 = 0.10 \times 500 = 50$.
13. **c) Both I and II follow** — Standard syllogism deduction.
14. **b) Painstaking / Careful** — Meticulous means highly attentive to detail.
15. **a) 3, 4** — $(x-3)(x-4) = 0 \implies x = 3, 4$.
