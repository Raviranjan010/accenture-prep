# Stage 5: Group Discussion (GD) Guide

## What this is
Certain Accenture hiring drives (specifically off-campus pool drives or special hiring tracks) include a **Group Discussion (GD) Round**. A panel of 8–12 candidates is given a topic (technology trend, ethical dilemma, or current affairs) and 10-15 minutes to conduct a structured group discussion. The panel evaluates communication skills, active listening, logical reasoning, team accommodation, and leadership dynamics.

---

## Formula / Rule / Pattern & Golden Rules

```
[Opening Anchor (Optional)] ──> [State Position & Frame Structure] ──> [Support with 2 Facts/Impacts] ──> [Include Quiet Participants / Summarize]
```

### 5 Golden Rules of GD Success
1. **Quality over Quantity**: Speaking 2–3 times with high-value logical points is superior to continuous loud interruption.
2. **Never Argue Aggressively**: Disagree respectfully using phrases like *"I see your point, however, another angle to consider is..."*.
3. **Bring Data / Structure**: Group the discussion into dimensions (e.g. *Economic Impact, Environmental Impact, Ethical Concerns*).
4. **Encourage Others**: Inviting a quiet candidate (*"Let's hear what Candidate 5 thinks about this aspect"*) displays executive leadership skills.
5. **Summarize Cleanly**: If the panel asks for a summary, synthesize both sides of the discussion objectively without taking personal credit.

## 10 High-Frequency GD Topics with Pro/Con Points

### Topic 1: Generative AI — Job Threat or Productivity Booster?
- **Pro Booster Points**:
  - Automates repetitive boilerplate code, allowing developers to focus on high-level architecture.
  - Creates new tech job roles: Prompt Engineers, AI Safety Officers, ML Ops Engineers.
- **Con / Threat Points**:
  - Entry-level coding roles face skill threshold shifts.
  - Requires rapid workforce upskilling to prevent displacement.
- **Balanced Consensus**: Gen AI is a powerful productivity multiplier, but requires policy safeguards and continuous workforce retraining.

---

### Topic 2: Work From Home vs. Return to Office
- **WFH Points**: Reduces commuting stress/emissions, improves work-life balance, expands talent pool globally.
- **Office Points**: Enhances team bonding, spontaneous whiteboarding collaboration, and cybersecurity compliance.
- **Consensus**: Hybrid work models offer the optimal balance for corporate IT organizations.

---

### Topic 3: Electric Vehicles (EVs) — Is India Ready for 100% Transition by 2030?
- **Pros**: Reduces oil import bills, decreases urban air pollution, promotes indigenous battery manufacturing.
- **Cons**: High initial cost, grid power strain, lack of fast-charging infrastructure in Tier-2/3 cities.

---

### Topic 4: Social Media — Fostering Connectivity or Increasing Isolation?
- **Pros**: Enables global community building, instant news dissemination, small business marketing.
- **Cons**: Screen addiction, mental health issues, echo chambers, misinformation propagation.

---

### Topic 5: Cashless Economy & UPI — Security Concerns vs Convenience
- **Pros**: Financial inclusion, transparent transactions, digital economy growth.
- **Cons**: Cyber fraud vulnerability, digital literacy gap in rural areas, server downtime risks.

---

### Topic 6: Cloud Computing — Data Sovereignty and Security Risks
- **Pros**: Scalability, cost reduction, high availability.
- **Cons**: Cross-border data privacy compliance, vendor lock-in, cloud outage vulnerability.

---

### Topic 7: Moonlighting (Dual Employment) — Ethical Dilemma or Developer Freedom?
- **Pros**: Income supplementation, skill enhancement outside work hours.
- **Cons**: Conflict of interest, IP leakage risks, worker burnout affecting primary employer productivity.

---

### Topic 8: Green Computing and Environmental Impact of Data Centers
- **Pros**: Transition to renewable energy, liquid cooling technology, carbon-neutral server architecture.
- **Cons**: High water consumption for cooling, massive electricity footprint of AI model training.

---

### Topic 9: Automation in Manufacturing and Healthcare
- **Pros**: Surgical precision, 24/7 error-free assembly lines, reduced human risk in hazardous jobs.
- **Cons**: Capital expenditure, loss of unskilled labor jobs.

---

### Topic 10: 5G & 6G Telecom Infrastructure — Economic Catalyst or Hype?
- **Pros**: Ultra-low latency, IoT smart cities, remote robotic surgery, industrial automation.
- **Cons**: High spectrum licensing costs, dense cell tower requirements.
