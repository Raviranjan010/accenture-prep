# Accenture Placement Preparation — Beginner's Master Roadmap

Welcome to the **Accenture Placement Preparation Master Roadmap**. This guide outlines the exact learning order, prerequisites, difficulty progression, practice tracking, and week-by-week milestones across all preparation tracks.

---

## 🧭 Learning Sequence

1. **00-start-here/** — Eligibility criteria, ATS resume guidelines, hardware & SEB logistics setup.
2. **01-gamified-assessment/** — Memory Maze, Path Finder, Quick-Fire Math, Classic Cognitive MCQ fallback.
3. **02-aptitude-reasoning/** — Quantitative, Logical Reasoning, Abstract Reasoning, Data Interpretation, Verbal.
4. **03-technical-assessment/** — CS Fundamentals (OOP, DBMS, OS, Networks, Cloud/Security, MS Office) & Pseudocode Trace Tables.
5. **04-coding-round/** — Core DSA & Language Practice Banks (Python, Java, C++, C, JavaScript, SQL).
6. **05-communication-assessment/** — Versant 6-Section Drills & Spoken Scripts.
7. **06-interview-prep/** — Technical Interview Defense, HR STAR Answers, Group Discussion Topics.
8. **07-mock-tests/** — Timed Mock Test Papers 1-3 & Capstone Assessment Simulation.
9. **08-progress-trackers/** — Performance Logs & Solved Problem Tracking.
